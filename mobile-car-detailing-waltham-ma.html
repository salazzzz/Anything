Detailing in Waltham, specifically.

Waltham is really several different parking situations wearing one name. Around Moody Street and the City Center it is apartments, condos and shared lots. Out in Piety Corner and Warrendale it is single-family homes with proper driveways and room to spare.

There is also a real student population here between Bentley and Brandeis — cars that sit through a New England winter taking road salt and then get sold or handed on in spring. A detail before that sale is usually worth several times what it costs.

Where we work in Waltham.

If you are in a condo or apartment near Moody Street, all we need is a spot we are allowed to occupy for the length of the job — a numbered space or visitor spot is fine. In Piety Corner, Warrendale and North Waltham there is normally a driveway, which is ideal. We carry our own water and power either way, so no hookup is needed.
